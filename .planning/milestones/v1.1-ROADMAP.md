**Success Criteria** (what is TRUE):

  1. `analyze_event` sweep detection (high/low sweep identification + reversal-vs-momentum-box resolution), `main.py` session-context/gap features, and `injection.py` lookup/range functions are all covered by passing direct tests on controlled fixtures — not just the loaders around them. *(TEST-01, TEST-02, TEST-03)*

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**

- Scope v1.1 to core-test coverage + the two real validity fixes + hygiene — no new research. Harden the foundation (the sweep kernel was ported untested in v1.0) before extending the idea.
- Execute v1.1 as direct GSD fixes (no per-phase discuss/plan/execute) — small, mechanical, well-understood tasks; matches the v1.0 precedent for mechanical phases.
- Treat the `loc`/`iloc` validity bug as already resolved by the polars migration (VALID-03 = confirm-and-close), since `get_candles_until_eod` was rewritten to pure-polars positional slicing in v1.0 and no `.loc`/`.iloc` remain repo-wide.

**Issues Resolved:**

- Hardcoded `16:59` ET prior-close magic minute eliminated (`get_last_candle_before`) — short/holiday sessions no longer silently miss (VALID-01).
- Silent event-dropping replaced by a counted, reported dropped-event summary line (`analyze_event` takes an optional `drops` Counter; `main()` prints coverage) (VALID-02).
- Over-broad global `warnings.filterwarnings("ignore")` narrowed to ConvergenceWarning / UndefinedMetricWarning around the emitting call (QUAL-01).
- 4 stale root-level PNGs removed; `/*.png` gitignored as chart-output policy (HYG-01).
- README brought current for the polars era (HYG-02).

**Technical Debt Incurred:** None new. Carried forward from v1.0:

- Shared utilities (`ensure_utc`, `find_sorted_pos`, `qcut_with_fallback_labels`) remain triplicated inline per script (STRUCT-01, deferred to a future structure milestone).
